What we fix

Repairs, replacements and small renovations for homes across Greater Victoria. If water goes through it, we work on it.

Leaks and burst pipes

Split or broken pipe found and repaired, and the water cleaned up behind it.

Blocked drains

Slow sinks, backed-up tubs and perimeter drains cleared and flowing again.

No hot water

Hot water tanks diagnosed, repaired or replaced, usually the same day.

Toilets and taps

Running toilets, dripping kitchen and bathroom faucets, and garden spigots.

Tubs and showers

Valves, diverters and fixtures repaired or swapped for something you like better.

Small renovations

Bathroom and kitchen refreshes, from a new vanity to the whole room re-plumbed.
